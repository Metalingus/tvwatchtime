TV Time alternative FAQ

Can I import my TV Time watch history?

Yes. TV Watch Time includes an importer for exported TV Time data. The import review screen lets you check detected matches before completing the process.

Is TV Watch Time free?

The hosted app is free to use, and the project is open source.

Does TV Watch Time track both shows and movies?

Yes. You can track episodes, shows and movies, maintain watchlists, rate titles and follow upcoming releases.

Is this the official TV Time website?

No. TV Watch Time is an independent TV-tracking project and is not affiliated with TV Time or Whip Media.
