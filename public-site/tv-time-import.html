TV Time import guide

How to import your TV Time data into TV Watch Time

Use your TV Time data archive to carry your viewing history into TV Watch Time instead of rebuilding it manually.

Open TV Watch TimeWhy switch?

Before you begin

Your TV Time export

Keep the archive as a ZIP file. Do not rename or edit the files inside unless the importer instructs you to do so.

Your TV Watch Time account

Sign in first so the imported history can be attached to the correct profile.

A few minutes to review

Titles can have regional names or multiple adaptations, so review suggested matches before confirming.

Import steps

Get your data archive from TV Time

Use the data export or privacy tools available in your TV Time account and download the resulting archive when it is ready.

Sign in to TV Watch Time

Open the hosted app and go to the import section in your account settings.

Upload the original ZIP file

Select the archive you downloaded. Keep the browser open while the file is uploaded and processed.

Review detected titles

Check the proposed matches for shows and movies. Pay particular attention to remakes, regional titles and anime seasons.

Confirm the import

Complete the operation, then verify your profile statistics, watched episodes and movie history.

After the import

Check progress

Open a few long-running shows and confirm that seasons and watched episodes look correct.

Review statistics

Some aggregate statistics may take a little time to recalculate after a large import.

Keep the original archive

Store the export safely until you have verified the imported data.

Common import questions

Should I unzip the TV Time archive first?

No. Upload the original ZIP archive unless the importer explicitly asks for an extracted file.

Why do I need to review matches?

Different services can use different names, season structures or identifiers for the same title. Review prevents the wrong adaptation or release from being imported.

Can I use TV Watch Time without importing?

Yes. The importer is optional; you can start with a new account and track titles manually.

Where can I report an import problem?

Use the project's GitHub issue tracker or join the TV Watch Time Discord community with the non-sensitive details of the error.

Do not share your export archive publicly. It may contain personal account and viewing-history data.
