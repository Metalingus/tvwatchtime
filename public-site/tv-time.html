TV Time alternative FAQ

Can I import my TV Time watch history?

Yes. Upload a supported TV Time export, review the detected matches, and confirm the data you want to bring into TV Watch Time.

What can TV Watch Time import from TV Time?

Depending on the export format and available data, imports can include watched episodes and movies, watchlists, favorites, custom lists, ratings, reactions and comments.

Does importing change my TV Time account?

No. TV Watch Time reads the exported archive and does not write changes back to TV Time.

Where can I use TV Watch Time?

TV Watch Time is available on iOS, Android and the web. It also supports personal self-hosted backends.

Is this the official TV Time website?

No. TV Watch Time is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with TV Time or Whip Media.
